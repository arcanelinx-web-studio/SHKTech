YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy & enquiries.

What stays in your browser

The enquiry list and item notes are stored in your browser’s localStorage. Clear the list or browser site data to remove them. On the interactive GitHub Pages showcase, submitted preview enquiries also stay only in that browser so the client can test the lead-desk experience. On the production Hostinger deployment, submitted contact and requirement details are stored for SHK so the team can respond and manage follow-up.

Sending a requirement

The form first submits your enquiry to SHK's lead desk. It can then generate a WhatsApp message containing the same requirement for you to review and send. If you opt in and SHK's WhatsApp Business messaging is configured, SHK may also send an acknowledgement and catalogue link using that service. WhatsApp's own privacy terms apply.

Drawings and attachments

When SHK's secure attachment storage is configured, supported drawings, photos and documents selected in the form are uploaded with the enquiry and are available only through the authenticated SHK lead desk. If that storage is unavailable, the site keeps the filenames in the enquiry and asks you to attach the files manually in WhatsApp.

Analytics and hosting

This build uses no analytics scripts or advertising cookies. The hosting provider may process ordinary request logs when the site is deployed.
